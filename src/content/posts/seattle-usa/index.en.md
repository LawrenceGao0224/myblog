# Day 3

On the first and third Saturdays of every month, the Amazon Spheres are open to the public. Today happened to be the third Saturday, so I went. Inside you can feel nature and the office blended perfectly, with an Amazon rainforest vibe.

![](./13.jpg)

![](./14.jpg)

There I met a Taiwanese couple. The husband is a professor at Taipei Medical University, and we talked a lot about how AI is shaping healthcare. I ended up tagging along with them for the next few stops.

![](./22.jpg)

After walking around the Space Needle, we headed to Kerry Park. The view there is top-notch, and the houses around it are all luxury homes.

# Thoughts

> The houses are beautiful, the roads are wide, and it's not too crowded. It's a backpacker-friendly place > Diverse and easy to get around, but I feel like it'd get a bit boring if you stayed too long 
> The downside is the streets are full of cigarette and weed smoke. If you stay Downtown you'll see drug users more often, but everyone's used to it and people basically leave each other alone 
> Unless you have a high salary, the cost of living here is a real burden 
> Also, lots of people skip paying on public transit here, but nobody really seems to check 
> And I made a shocking discovery here: I'm even more of an E than Americans!
