# Itinerary

This time we signed up for a 5-day Cebu tour from Wufu Travel (五福旅行社) that we found at a travel fair. Here's the detailed itinerary: [click here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1f0hoP7dbMwaaEiY8QvUAGSwADEi2dTjg/view?usp=sharing)

Since we came across it at the travel fair, we saw the cheap NT$13,900 price and decided to book it on the spot

## Day 1 
We landed in Cebu around noon on the first day, but waiting for our checked luggage took forever — I almost thought it hadn't made it...
Our group had 12 people. After meeting the guide and everyone else, the guide took us to our first stop, **SM city**, to exchange money and have lunch. The exchange rate here was the best I saw
![](./1.jpg)

For lunch we had Filipino food. Some people in the group thought it was too salty, and I did find some dishes a bit salty too, but I thought most of it was pretty tasty (maybe I'm just easy to please XD)

After that we visited Magellan's Cross (the cross the explorer Magellan left here), Fort San Pedro, and the Basilica del Santo Niño
![](./2.jpg)
![](./3.jpg)

Afterward we went straight back to the hotel to check in, then headed to [Ayala Center Cebu](https://maps.app.goo.gl/y8SdNCssSoyCBXUe8) next door for dinner. We tried a highly rated Italian restaurant — it was good, but honestly pretty pricey, and it's the kind of food you can get in Taiwan, so I won't recommend it here

After walking around the mall, we went back to the hotel to rest

## Day 2

Up at 6:30am for the hotel breakfast buffet
This day we took a small boat out to go diving near Nalusuan Island (資生堂島), ate seafood on the island, and tried SUP. There were tons of fish around, but honestly the island wasn't as beautiful as I'd imagined, so I didn't take many photos
![](./4.jpg)
![](./5.jpg)
![](./6.jpg)

Back at the hotel, we went to a nearby restaurant, [House of Lechon](https://maps.app.goo.gl/hLcxix9L3wbrfJS18), for dinner. The roast suckling pig was amazing, and the Halo halo (their dessert) was so good too, though it was on the pricey side. After eating, we wandered around Ayala Center Cebu and then went back to the hotel
![](./7.jpg)

## Day 3

This was the highlight of the trip: whale sharks! We had to get up at 2:30am and meet at 3, and no sunscreen allowed — but it was my favorite part of the whole trip
The whale shark spot is in [Oslob](https://maps.app.goo.gl/aXxS3z69naoUFhDc7), over 3 hours' drive away, and once you get there you still have to line up for a number, but it was so worth it
![](./8.jpg)
Its tail brushed me 3 times, but you're not allowed to touch it yourself

After leaving the whale shark area, our whole group agreed to pay extra to see the sardine run, since it's on the way and supposedly a really worthwhile experience
It's at a snorkeling spot on the west coast, roughly [here](https://maps.app.goo.gl/uTQ64uUjA32vDrro9)
Here you can see sea turtles and an insane number of sardines
![](./9.jpg)
![](./10.jpg)

But there weren't as many turtles as I saw in Xiaoliuqiu, which was a little disappointing — the sardines were awesome though

We got back to the hotel around 6, then went to Ayala Center Cebu for Filipino food. We ordered way too much and were stuffed
![](./11.jpg)

## Day 4

Today we went to Bohol. We'd found our own local guide to take us around for the whole day. We called a Grab to Pier 1 and had booked the ferry to Bohol ourselves on KKday. We heard business class was great, so we booked it — and I slept like a log on board
![](./19.jpg)


Here's the order of our itinerary:

Personally, I didn't think it was great value. With the boat tickets, it came to over NT$10,000 for two of us, but everything we did was stuff I'd already experienced elsewhere, plus it felt a bit rushed, so it didn't feel quite worth it. Then again, if I hadn't come, I'd probably have left with regrets. Maybe I'm just more into water activities...

We took the ferry back to Cebu and had their fast food, Jollibee, for dinner — honestly pretty good and cheap

At night we had our last massage at the hotel. So relaxing and so cheap, I wish I could get one every day...
![](./21.jpg)

## Day 5

After breakfast at the hotel, we drove to the airport and flew back to Taiwan...

# Total Cost

Guide fee: 3000
Sardine run (add-on): US$100
Currency exchange: US$300
Meals: 1000+1200
Dried mangoes: 1500
Taxi (home in Taipei to Taoyuan Airport): 800
Ferry tickets: 4800
Jollibee : 150
Snacks (souvenirs): 727
Massage: 800
Photos (basically buying photos from the local guide as a way of tipping): 400
Tour fee: 13900/person

**Total**: 54177 (for two)
