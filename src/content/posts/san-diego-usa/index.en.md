# Summary

The weather in San Diego is super pleasant, and it feels pretty safe. It's not just a tourist hub but a business hub too. I met lots of people here for conferences. Since the ocean is so close, you can slow down and enjoy the sea breeze every now and then. But prices are high, and you see a lot of wealthy people on the streets. Yet on the other side of downtown there are many homeless people in makeshift tents. If you have enough money, living here really would be paradise on earth.
