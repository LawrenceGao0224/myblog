# Things to Consider Before the Trip

While planning the places we wanted to visit and how to get around, we realized there were only four counties/cities (Hualien, Taitung, Kaohsiung, Tainan) we really wanted to go to. Plus it was winter, when the northeast monsoon brings heavy rain to the north. So we decided to do half the trip by train and half by scooter, which let us spend more time in Hualien and Taitung and also avoid the risk of rain along the northeast coast

# Route

# Takeaways

The whole round-island trip cost NT$13,533 in total, with most of it going to vehicle rentals and transportation.
After working for over a year, things fell into place and I finally made my long-unfulfilled dream of traveling around Taiwan happen — a spur-of-the-moment trip. We didn't make it to every city, but that actually let us explore each place more deeply. Especially in Yuli, Hualien: we hadn't planned to spend a night there, but a friend told us Yuli noodles are a must-eat, so we stopped there. We got to know the guesthouse owner, who shares the same passion as me — actions speak louder than words. We also tried Yuli noodles (they seem a lot like plain yangchun noodles but are made differently — I couldn't taste the difference) and the amazing local Mida (米達) charcoal-grilled chicken cutlet. Sometimes life is more exciting precisely because of the unexpected — by chance you discover joys that are hard to find. On every patch of land in this world, everyone lives out their own life in their own way.
