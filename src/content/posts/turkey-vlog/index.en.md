# Preparation
1. US$500~1,000 and a MasterCard or Visa credit card (most places take cards, except street stalls)
2. European plug adapter
3. SIM card
4. Winter clothes (layers), since a lot of indoor spaces are heated but it's -5~5°C outside. I brought Uniqlo Heattech
5. Passport and visa
6. Personal medication
7. Instant noodles are up to you. I wanted to try the local food and didn't want them taking up luggage space
8. Power bank
9. Sunscreen and sunglasses
10. Skincare

# How to Haggle

At the Grand Bazaar, vendors always start with a high price. Check whether it's way off from what you expected. If something you think is worth 100 is offered at 1,000 right off the bat, just move on to the next shop, haha. At other shops, start a bit below the price you have in mind. For example, if they ask US$300, you can start at US$120. Yep, that big a gap. After some back-and-forth, you can usually get it down by half or even more.
