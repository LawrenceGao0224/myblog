# Lessons Learned

On the last day, when we returned the rental car, we found a scrape from someone bumping into it. The rental company said they'd wait for a mechanic to give a quote, and had us leave an ID plus a NT$3,000 deposit so we could catch our flight, saying they'd message us later and settle the difference either way. By the time we got to the airport, they quoted us NT$12,700, claiming red paint is more expensive and pressuring us with "island prices." We were furious. A friend asked a repair shop they knew, who said the quote was kind of unreasonable, so we kept negotiating with the owner. We were willing to pay for the damage, but the price just didn't make sense.
