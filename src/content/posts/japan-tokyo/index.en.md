# Preparation

Flights: Double-check everything when you book, especially the name spelling and passport number. I typed my girlfriend's name wrong, and we didn't notice until the day before the flight.

Hotels: The good hotels fill up fast, so we used [booking.com](http://booking.com) and [agoda.com](http://agoda.com) to compare prices and book.

Weather: It was really hot when we arrived, but three days later it got pretty chilly, around 20°C.

Clothing & necessities:

[What to Pack for Japan: 8 Essential Things for a Hassle-Free Trip | LIVE JAPAN travel guide](https://livejapan.com/en/article-a0000218/)

Our plane took off at 14:25 and landed at 18:50.

## Day 2 : Kamakura & Enoshima

Kamakura is a cool coastal town just an hour from Tokyo by train. We kicked off at 8:00 AM, and the streets were still pretty quiet.

We stopped at a cute bakery inside Shinagawa Station for some yummy mini sandwiches. Can you believe how fresh and tasty they were? Oh, and remember, no eating while walking in Japan, so we sat down to enjoy them.

By 10 AM we hit Kamakura. It was kind of cloudy, but it felt nice. As soon as you step out of the station, you'll spot a torii gate marking the start of the famous [Komachi-dori shopping street](https://maps.app.goo.gl/inCCR8xKa67w1hC5A).

![](./4.jpg)

We tried matcha ice cream for the first time in Japan, and it was super tasty on a chilly day like this!

The shops here were adorable, selling local goodies like scents, crystal jewelry, honey, and more.
![](./13.jpg)
And don't miss the Good Luck Buddha cake. It's fluffy with a sweet filling, and each flavor brings luck in a different area of life – and they're delicious!
![](./5.jpg)

Further down, we came across [Tsurugaoka Hachimangu Shrine](https://maps.app.goo.gl/dkJHCtaJ58rjzaW69). It had this ancient, serene vibe that made us feel so calm.

Next up was the Great Buddha at [Kotoku-in Temple](https://maps.app.goo.gl/vbAeADHkU4mA5WuT9). This spot is a must-see, full of atmosphere and history that make it totally special.
![](./6.jpg)

Then I tried some sweet soy sauce dumplings. The owner was super kind and even treated us to some free matcha tea.

If you're into anime, you might recognize the famous [Kamakurakokomae crossing](https://maps.app.goo.gl/XqBm51KXiKMDJLnx9). We got there at noon, so getting a pic without someone's head in it was a bit of a challenge. Tip: for awesome pics, go early on a weekday!
![](./7.jpg)

I was starving, so we hit a local ramen shop, [Enoshima Black Ramen MENGA](https://maps.app.goo.gl/hKNvHcizGKchriF77). My girlfriend went for the beef rice and I got soy sauce ramen – both hit the spot.


Finally, we wrapped up the day at [Enoshima Island](https://maps.app.goo.gl/HcXBxzkaZze8rb767) right around sunset. The view was absolutely breathtaking! The island is connected by a bridge and has so much to explore. There are tons of souvenir shops by the torii gates and along the road leading to Enoshima Shrine. And trust me, the treats here are unique and totally worth a try!
![](./8.jpg)
![](./9.jpg)


Back in Ueno, we had grilled eel over rice for dinner, and it was really delicious. Let's call it a day!
![](./11.jpg)
![](./10.jpg)

## Day 3 : Ginza & Teamlab

We went to the [Kokyo Higashi Gyoen (East Gardens of the Imperial Palace)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/PN9RiVsrxWGHEPGX7) near Tokyo Station. It's a super cool and quiet place, but we couldn't actually go inside the palace, so we just strolled around the garden.
![](./14.jpg)
![](./15.jpg)

Around 10 in the morning, we went to Ginza and explored the area. At certain times the main street is pedestrian-only, with no cars allowed. It's full of upscale stores and famous restaurants. We checked out Uniqlo and MUJI, and let me tell you, they have everything you could want. I even bought some clothes there – it's a shopping paradise.
![](./17.jpg)
![](./19.jpg)

For lunch, we tried some local ramen at [Ginza Kousi (銀座 嚆矢)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/VuGF1eRXcUuzV2vY9). The soup was quite rich and I loved it, but my girlfriend found it a bit too salty.
![](./16.jpg)

Lastly, we visited [TeamLab Planets](https://maps.app.goo.gl/LL7ML45QgBjLUuDj9). Make sure you book tickets ahead of time, or you'll be waiting in line forever. It was crowded, but completely worth it. You have to immerse yourself in the whole experience, and it's kind of mysterious and cool. You can feel the lights and sounds throughout your whole body, even the ground, the water, and the floor. It was my first time experiencing anything like that, and it felt like a complete miracle.
![](./18.jpg)


## Day 4 : Harajuku, Shinjuku + Shibuya

We arrived at [Meiji Jingu (明治神宮)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/m3KBL9d2bpvWaz3j9) at about 9 in the morning. It was tranquil yet bustling with people, and the shrine itself radiates peace. It's dedicated to the emperor of the **Meiji Restoration**, which aimed to merge "modern advances" with traditional "Eastern" values.
![](./24.jpg)

Around 10:30, we checked out some thrift stores in Harajuku. I didn't buy any clothes, but I'd definitely recommend a visit. There are some cool streets and hidden spots in that area.
![](./20.jpg)

Lunchtime! We had sushi at this somewhat hidden place, [Sanaka](https://maps.app.goo.gl/M7ZXdKsVyo7PAztS7). It's a small restaurant, but the food is fantastic for the price. We didn't have to wait long, but by the time we left there was a line forming outside.
![](./21.jpg)

Later, we swung by Shibuya around 3:30. There were so many people – it felt like the whole world was crossing the [Shibuya Scramble Crossing](https://maps.app.goo.gl/NnqAayUgA52uWddB6). We visited some stores, including the Disney Store, and I ended up finding some affordable shoes at ABC-Mart. We'd planned to go up Shibuya Sky, but it was packed, so we just relaxed at the Starbucks right above the crossing. Watching everyone pass by was actually pretty fun.
![](./22.jpg)

We headed back to Ueno and had some tasty, affordable pork cutlets at [Tonkatsu Yamabe Ueno (とんかつ山家 上野店)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/h6yDvymHHUDTseWN6)
. That pretty much wrapped up our day.
![](./23.jpg)


## Day 5: Mount Fuji + Kabukicho + Drugstore

We joined a KKday tour that took us from Shinjuku to Mount Fuji. The package included tickets, a tour guide, and transportation, but not food or drinks.

Our first stop was [Arakurayama Sengen Park](https://maps.app.goo.gl/gY3JSubaG6kecdh4A), just a 5–10 minute walk from [Shimoyoshida Station](https://maps.app.goo.gl/ktMk67rKct4E6UUU7). I was captivated by the view from the shrine, even though the top of Fuji was hidden by clouds and there was no snow. It didn't look as spectacular as the photos I'd seen.
![](./25.jpg)

Next, we hit up [Shimoyoshida Honcho Street (Fuji Scenic View)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/J23EcGzJgiT2jUncA)
, a famous photo spot. But with so many tourists in the road, it's getting tough for the locals. Let's all follow the rules and not bother the people who live here. We managed to snap a few pics, and luckily the clouds didn't cover Fuji's peak.
![](./26.jpg)

For lunch, we went to [Oshino Hakkai](https://maps.app.goo.gl/RhaUr8DKgH8Yhoqd6), which has a famous pond that's 8 meters deep and super clear. We had sweet soy sauce dumplings, ice cream, and grilled beef.
![](./27.jpg)

Our final stop was [Saiko Iyashi no Sato Nenba](https://maps.app.goo.gl/iPWi6cT6P3z6DghAA), famous for its Gassho-zukuri thatched houses. We checked out some shops and strolled through the village. It was so peaceful and made us feel grateful.
![](./28.jpg)

We got back to Shinjuku before 5:30 PM and took a walk through [Kabukicho](https://maps.app.goo.gl/KZ4CVhbDJwPjj3XH9). We found a drugstore and went on a shopping spree, spending nearly 40,000 JPY.
![](./29.jpg)

For dinner, we picked [Gyukatsu Motomura (Shinjuku ALTA Back Street branch)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/AF3XBq9wPE68955G7). We waited about an hour, but it was totally worth it. The beef was crispy outside and tender inside, and you cook it to your liking on the little hot plate in front of you. Super yummy.

I loved this tour because it saved us the hassle of booking transport from Tokyo to Mount Fuji and planning our day around the sights.

Since it gets very crowded, I'd suggest arriving early. The gates started opening around 8 AM. I strongly recommend heading straight for the popular rides as soon as you're in, because if you spend too much time shopping or wandering, you'll end up waiting forever. The dessert was delicious, but the egg sandwich was just okay for me.

![](./50.jpg)
![](./51.jpg)

And then we headed to the airport, ready to say goodbye to Japan.

# Total Cost and Summary

The total cost was 106,815 NTD, with an exchange rate of about 1 JPY to 0.217 NTD.

This was my first time in Japan, and everything felt so new to me. Experiencing teamLab, DisneySea, and Mt. Fuji was exciting. But somehow the style of the streets and the atmosphere made me feel like I'd been living here for 27 years.

Everyone is polite and well-dressed. I could also tell people are considerate – they read the room and try not to cause anyone trouble.

Japan is a foreigner-friendly country: people are welcoming, and the train signs are clear and convenient.
