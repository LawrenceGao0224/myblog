> Kyoto blends old and new with so much charm
> Osaka is packed with shopping streets and shrines
> Nara is all about the deer and the shopping streets
> Kobe, a big port opened up during the Meiji Restoration, has a strong international vibe. Kobe beef is a must

# Preparation
For packing, you can check out this [luggage list](https://www.e-japannavi.com/info/info1_4.shtml)

From the airport we headed to Kyoto and went straight to the Airbnb to check in and drop off our bags

## Day 3 Kyoto

We set off early for [Arashiyama](https://maps.app.goo.gl/FniDU9RTAHC7UNbB8). The scenery around there is beautiful. We had udon for lunch nearby, and there are shopping streets on both sides to browse. We stayed until 14:00
![](./11.jpg)
![](./12.jpg)

In the afternoon we went to [Nijo Castle](https://maps.app.goo.gl/rraXP2eTKV6gccuy6). Just remember that last entry is at 4 p.m. We were literally the last ones let in. Photos aren't allowed inside, but you get to learn how the samurai in the castle held their meetings and how the buildings were laid out. Worth a visit if you're into Japanese history
![](./13.jpg)
![](./14.jpg)

And that wraps up the Kyoto part of the trip~~~.

We took the train back to Osaka to check in to our Airbnb, then went out to eat with my girlfriend and her sister at [Gyukatsu Motomura Namba](https://maps.app.goo.gl/YRpTb4K7fdWi35tD8). We browsed the shopping streets nearby until 11 and then went back to rest

# Total Cost

For two people, everything added up to about NT$81,000. Accommodation was around NT$1,000 per night, round-trip China Airlines tickets for two were NT$28,000, and two USJ tickets were about NT$3,800. Everything else (drugstore stuff, snacks, souvenirs, meals, etc.) depends on the person

# Summary

For shopping, I personally prefer Tokyo, but for sightseeing and the pace of life, I love the vibe of Kyoto and Osaka. Accommodation in Kyoto and Osaka is also cheaper than Tokyo, and the rooms are way bigger.
There were 12 of us in our group this time. My girlfriend and I came 4 days early before our friends arrived. Staying in an Airbnb together and hanging out with friends abroad felt amazing.
